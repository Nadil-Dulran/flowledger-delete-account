FlowLedger — Account and Data Deletion Request

Last updated: October 21, 2026

About account deletion

FlowLedger is a personal finance management app that helps users manage income, expenses, recurring bills, savings goals and financial summaries.

Users can request deletion of their FlowLedger account and associated personal data by following the instructions below.

How to request account deletion

To request deletion of your FlowLedger account:

  1. Send an email to hello@tetrovatechnologies.com.
  2. Use the subject line FlowLedger Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include the email address associated with your FlowLedger account so we can identify the account.
  4. We may contact you to verify ownership of the account before processing the request.

You do not need to provide your password or other sensitive authentication credentials.

What data will be deleted?

Once the request is verified and processed, we will delete the account and associated data that can be deleted, subject to applicable legal obligations and technical limitations. This may include:

  • Account information, such as your registered email address and authentication-linked account data.
  • Financial records stored in FlowLedger, including income, expenses and transaction descriptions.
  • Recurring bill records and savings goals.
  • Cloud-synchronized financial data associated with your account, where applicable.

Financial records stored only on your device may need to be deleted separately by removing them within the app or clearing the app's local data.

Data that may be retained

Certain information may need to be retained where required by law, for security purposes or to resolve disputes. If any information must be retained, it will be limited to what is necessary for the relevant purpose and retained only for the required period.

Copies in backups may remain until they are removed through the applicable backup retention process.

Processing time

We will review and process verified deletion requests within a reasonable period, subject to applicable legal requirements. If any data must be retained, we will explain the applicable reason and retention period where required.
